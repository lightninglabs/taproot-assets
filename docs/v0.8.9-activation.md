# v0.8.9 activation

tapd v0.8.9 changes how asset transfers are proven. From an activation
height, upgraded nodes accept only transfers made the new way. Transfers
that confirm before it stay valid.

## Activation heights

- Mainnet: block 978,493, expected around 2026-12-02.
- Signet, with the default challenge: block 331,000, expected around
  2026-11-15.
- Testnet3: block 5,360,000.
- Testnet4: block 164,500.

## Before the activation height

1. Upgrade every node to v0.8.9, including universe servers.
2. Don't send assets from an earlier version close to or past the
   activation height. A transfer made by an earlier version that
   confirms at or past it can't be proven to upgraded nodes, and its
   assets, change included, are lost.

Earlier versions can't receive transfers made by v0.8.9. Such a receive
waits, and completes once the node upgrades.

## Asset channels

Close every asset channel before the activation height, and reopen it
once both peers run v0.8.9:

1. Settle or fail any HTLCs on the channel.
2. Close the channel, cooperatively if the peer is online, so that the
   closing transaction confirms, not merely broadcasts, before the
   activation height.
3. Once both peers run v0.8.9, open a new channel. tapd v0.8.9 opens
   asset channels only with peers that also run v0.8.9.

Don't upgrade a node while a channel update is in flight, as the
channel may then be force closed.

An asset channel opened before v0.8.9 that is still open at the
activation height can lose its assets and its BTC: on a force close, a
cooperative close, or a revoked state broadcast by a peer. From the
activation height, tapd v0.8.9 sends no asset payments over a channel
with a peer on an earlier version.

## After the activation height

Upgraded nodes reject transfers made the old way that confirm at or
past the activation height.
